01Inside the world

Places to explore.

You start on the shore. Signposts point to the lake, the mosque, the museum, the garden, the caravanserai and the prison, and ten stargates reach further still — from Masjid al-Haram and the Burj Khalifa to Petra and Hegra.

02Learn by building

Seven missions.

Each mission floats a glowing blueprint in the world. Pick the right block, tap the highlighted cubes to fill it in, and the next set unlocks. The maths is the build.

  1. 01The WellCount around a square: a ring with three blocks on each side needs eight.8 blocks
  2. 02Garden WallMeasure the edge, then fill it — area and perimeter in one wall.18 blocks
  3. 03The GateBuild a mirror line so the left side matches the right: symmetry.13 blocks
  4. 04Tiled CourtyardTiles repeat in patterns. A five by five square holds twenty-five.25 blocks
  5. 05The MinaretVolume is length × width × height. Try it on a tower.33 blocks
  6. 06The DomeA dome can roof a wide room with no pillar in the middle.49 blocks
  7. 07House of WisdomSquare numbers and careful counting — the last blueprint.26 blocks

03Drone attack · Open Air Prison

Shelter twenty-four.

A solid roof two to eight blocks above a floor hides one person, so a bigger shelter saves more. Anyone caught in the open is knocked down and gets up again.

Five star tiers, no deaths to score, and a scoreboard that only counts the people you got under cover.

  • 24 people
  • 5 star tiers
  • 0 deaths

04Museum of Islamic Heritage

The museum.

Four floors of cases: astrolabes, clocks, calligraphy tools and water-raising machines. Fifteen exhibits can show a real museum photograph over the drawing — and offline, the drawing simply stays.

08Play free

Plays in the browser.

One offline HTML file — raw WebGL, no libraries. Touch on phones and tablets, mouse and keyboard on desktop, and adaptive quality so older devices keep up.

No ads, no chat, no tracking. Progress and every block you place stay saved on your own device.
